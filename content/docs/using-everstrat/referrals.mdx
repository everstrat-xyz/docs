Referrals let registered wallets invite friends to Everstrat with a reusable personal code.
A friend becomes your direct referral when they accept your invitation and the app verifies
their wallet signature. They do not need to deposit ETH first.

<Callout type="info" title="Referral earnings are not available yet">
  **Referral earnings** shows **Coming soon**. The implementation does not calculate rewards,
  maintain an earnings balance, or pay referral commissions. The referral count records
  registrations, not deposits or rewards.
</Callout>

## How it works

<DocsDiagram name="referrals" />

Accepting an invite creates an app registration and browser session. It does not send ETH,
mint EVE, or complete an on-chain whitelist transaction. A deposit is a separate action with
its own risk acknowledgment, access checks, quote, and gas cost. See
[Your first deposit](/get-started/first-mint).

## Share your invite

1. Open **Portfolio** and connect the wallet you registered with.
2. Find the **Referrals** card below your position and account cards. If prompted, select
   **Sign in with wallet** and sign the sign-in message.
3. Your five-character **Your invite code** is shown in the card. Select **Copy invite link**
   to copy the full link on the current app domain, with the path `/invite/<CODE>`.
4. Send the link to your friend. They can also open `/invite` and enter the code manually.

The same personal code can register multiple friends; it has no registration limit.
It stays assigned to your wallet and cannot be edited or regenerated. Codes are public
invitations; knowing a code does not grant access to its owner's account.

## Join through a friend

1. Open your friend's invite link. The invite form fills in their code automatically.
2. Connect the wallet you want to register and switch to **Ethereum mainnet** if requested.
3. Select **Accept invite** and review the wallet message. The referral message states
   **Join Everstrat through a referral.** Accepting the invitation is a message signature
   with no transaction gas fee.
4. Once verification succeeds, the app opens **Portfolio**. You have your own personal
   code and appear in your friend's **Your referrals** list.

Opening the link or connecting a wallet alone does not record a referral. If you cancel
the signature, you can try again. Signature challenges expire after five minutes;
start a new attempt if yours expires.

If your wallet is already registered, choose **Already a member? Sign in** instead.
Signing in restores access without changing who invited you.

## Attribution rules

- **First registration fixes attribution.** A registered wallet cannot join again under
  another personal code. There is no app action to change its referrer.
- **Only direct invitations count.** If Alice invites Bob and Bob invites Carol, Alice sees
  Bob; Bob sees Carol. Carol is not added to Alice's list.
- **No self-referrals.** You cannot register your own wallet with its own code.
- **Registration is per wallet and deployment.** A wallet address is not proof of a unique
  person. Referral counts do not measure unique people or funded accounts.
- **Administrative invitations have no referrer.** Wallets registered through administrative
  invites receive personal codes without assigning the issuing administrator as their referrer.

Both administrative and personal codes use the same invite form. A personal code is reusable;
an administrative code is claimed by one wallet. Personal acceptance opens Portfolio, while
administrative invite acceptance opens Mint.

## Read your referral list

**Your referrals** shows the total number of direct registrations, each wallet's explorer
link, and its **Joined (UTC)** date. The list shows the newest registrations first, ten per
page. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move through it, or **Refresh referrals** to return to
the first page and load new registrations.

Only the signed-in wallet can load its list. Switching wallets may require another sign-in.
The list does not expose invitees' emails, deposits, balances, or earnings.

## Common questions

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A friend opened the link but is missing from the list | Ask them to finish **Accept invite** and wallet verification, then refresh your list. A visit alone does not count. |
| The wallet is already registered | Use **Already a member? Sign in**. A different invitation cannot replace its attribution. |
| Portfolio asks you to sign in again | Select **Sign in with wallet** for the connected registered wallet. Connecting a wallet and signing in are separate steps. |
| Referral data fails to load | Select **Try again** or refresh. An unavailable list is not a zero referral count. |
| You want to claim referral earnings | Reward calculations and payouts are not implemented. There is no referral claim action. |
