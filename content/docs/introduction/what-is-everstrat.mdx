<Callout type="warn" title="Testing phase — treat as a test protocol">
  Everstrat is deployed on **Ethereum mainnet** (chain id 1), but it is in a **testing phase**.
  Entry is invite-gated, the software is experimental and unaudited, and deposits are real ETH
  at real risk. Do not deposit more than you can afford to lose entirely.
</Callout>

Everstrat lets you deposit ETH and receive **EVE**, a token representing your share of a managed,
on-chain strategy portfolio. Keepers coordinate routine strategy operations. Governance controls
which strategies are registered and how they are configured.

EVE's value tracks the portfolio's **net asset value (NAV)**. Strategies can earn or lose value;
there is no promised return. To exit, you redeem EVE for ETH through the protocol's native market.
Redemption depends on pause state, available valuation, liquidity, and queue conditions.

## The core loop

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Deposit ETH and mint EVE.** The app estimates how much EVE your deposit will receive at
    the entry price. You review the amount, slippage, and risks before confirming in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Hold EVE.** Its underlying value changes with the strategy portfolio. You can review your
    position and pending redemptions on **Portfolio**, activity in the wallet account dialog,
    and protocol information on **Metrics**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Redeem EVE for ETH.** At the [dated snapshot](/operations/current-deployments#exit-liquidity-at-this-block),
    new exits queue. The AMM holds your EVE in escrow while the request is pending. After
    processing, you submit a separate **Claim** transaction to withdraw credited ETH.
    [The redemption queue](/how-everstrat-works/redemption-queue) has the full rules.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Naming

The product is **Everstrat**, and its share token's symbol is **EVE**. The deployed mainnet token
reports the name **Everstrat**. The older name **Everything Strategy** remains in infrastructure
identifiers such as the Goldsky subgraph slug `everything-strategy-mainnet`.
See [EVE contract reference](/developers/contracts/eve) for implementation and deployment details.

## Risks and limits

EVE is not a stablecoin, a fixed-APY product, or a guarantee of principal protection. Funds are
held in protocol contracts and managed strategies, with privileged roles and upgradeable
components. There is no insurance protecting deposits. Keepers, liquidity, and valuation
availability affect redemptions.

Read [Key risks](/risk-and-trust/key-risks), [Trust & governance](/risk-and-trust/trust-and-governance),
and the [Risk Disclosure](/legal/risk-disclosure) before using the app.
