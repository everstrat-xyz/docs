## What you are trusting

You rely on contract correctness, governance decisions, accurate NAV reporting, and
available strategy and queue automation. The timelock provides advance visibility,
not principal protection or a guaranteed opportunity to exit before an upgrade.

## Role model

Governance configures and upgrades the management layer. SECURITY has designated emergency
powers, and keeper executors coordinate strategy work and queue processing.
The canonical [Roles and permissions](/risk-and-trust/roles-and-permissions) page defines each
role, registered-contract checks, and their limits. Current assignments live in the
[dated role snapshot](/operations/current-deployments#role-holders).

## The 48-hour timelock

At the snapshot, ADMIN is held by the timelock and `getMinDelay()` is **48 hours**.
A scheduled operation must mature and then be executed. "Ready" does not mean "Done";
a merged proposal or signed Safe transaction does not prove execution.

The Security Safe can cancel timelock operations under its CANCELLER permission.
Immediate SECURITY paths do not wait for the ADMIN delay.

The review window is **not an exit guarantee**. An exit can be paused, depend on NAV
availability, or need a queue that takes longer than the timelock delay. Cancellation
returns EVE and pays no ETH.

## SECURITY limits and accounting effects

SECURITY can remove a supported ERC-20 from NAV accounting without recovering that asset,
and revoke a Whitelist signer while the gate is enabled. These powers can change reported
backing or access. See [Emergency authority](/risk-and-trust/roles-and-permissions#emergency-authority)
for authorization, pause requirements, and limits. AMM claims and eligible cancellations remain
available during pauses.

## Immutable versus upgradeable

| Component | Code model |
| --- | --- |
| Registry, EVE, AMM, Whitelist, keeper executors | Static deployed code, with configurable permissions or settings |
| Each UniCL strategy | Immutable instance; replacement uses new deployment and registration |
| Controller, StrategyManager, ExitQueue, Oracle, Converter | ADMIN-authorized UUPS upgrades |

<span id="design-tradeoff" className="block scroll-m-28" />

Immutable AMM code still depends on configurable peers and upgradeable NAV/queue contracts.
Immutable strategy code still depends on feeds, routes, and external pools.
Fixed bytecode does not make economic behavior independent of governance.

## Operational trust

Strategy and queue automation depend on keeper availability, configured permissions, gas,
and reliable valuation inputs. Off-chain callers and on-chain executors have distinct
permissions; see [Keepers and automation](/developers/contracts/keepers-and-automation).

App operators must keep network and deployment configuration aligned with the contracts.
Indexing operators maintain the Goldsky deployment and its configuration. Account history
is eventually consistent, while balances and action eligibility are checked through RPC.
The app assumes Ethereum mainnet and handles unsupported networks explicitly.
See [Data access](/developers/data/subgraph-and-data-access) and
[Failure and recovery scenarios](/operations/failure-and-recovery-scenarios).
