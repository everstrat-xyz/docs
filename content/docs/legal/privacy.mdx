## The short version

We collect very little, and most of it is optional.

- Analytics load only if you accept them. Decline and nothing is sent.
- We never send your wallet address to any analytics service.
- The YouTube videos on the roadmap do not contact Google until you press play.
- If you give us your email for launch updates, we keep the email. We do not keep your IP address.
- If you accept an app invite, we store your wallet address against that invite, including its email address when one is attached.

## What we collect

### Analytics, only with your consent

We use Google Analytics 4 on all three sites to understand how people find the project and which parts of it they read.

Nothing is loaded from Google and no cookies are set until you press **Accept** on the consent banner. If you decline, or ignore the banner, no analytics cookies are written and no analytics data is sent.

If you accept, Google Analytics receives:

- the pages you visit and when
- the website or campaign link that sent you, including any `utm_` parameters
- approximate location, derived from your IP address and no more precise than city level
- device type, browser, operating system and screen size
- a randomly generated identifier stored in the `_ga` cookies, which lets us tell a returning visitor from a new one
- specific actions: opening the Everstrat app, playing a roadmap video, opening the docs, and following a link to X, Telegram or GitHub

Google discards your full IP address after deriving the approximate location. It is not stored in our reports and we never see it.

**We do not send Google your wallet address, your email, or anything that identifies you personally.** No page on either site puts a wallet address in its URL, so none can leak that way.

Google acts as our processor for this data. Google's own handling is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Launch updates email

If you enter your email address to be told when Everstrat launches, we store:

- the address as you typed it, and a lower-cased copy used to prevent duplicates
- the date and time you submitted it
- a note that it came from the launch form
- a one-way keyed hash of your IP address and of your browser's user agent string

The last point is worth being precise about. We do **not** store your IP address or your user agent. We store an HMAC-SHA256 hash of each, computed with a secret key held on the server. The hashes let us spot one source submitting hundreds of addresses. They cannot be reversed into an IP address, and we cannot use them to look you up.

When you submit the form, your email address is also posted to a private Telegram group used by the team, so that someone sees the signup. Telegram receives the address, the time and the signup source. Telegram's handling of that message is governed by the [Telegram Privacy Policy](https://telegram.org/privacy).

We use the launch list to tell you when Everstrat launches. If an app invite is created for
your email address, that address is also included in the invite record described below.
We do not sell or rent your email address or add it to unrelated mailing lists.

### Invite access and session cookie

Parts of app.everstrat.xyz are restricted while the project is in testing. To accept an invite,
you provide the invite code and sign a wallet message to prove control of your wallet.
We store your wallet address, the invite identifier and status, and the time the invite was
claimed. If the invite has an email address attached, this links that email to your wallet.
We use this record to manage invite access and prevent an invite being claimed by another wallet.

During verification, we also store a temporary challenge record linking your wallet to the
invite. It is consumed when verification succeeds; expired challenges are cleaned up when
new challenges are issued.

After verification, we set a cookie named `eve_invite_access` that lasts fourteen days.
It holds a random session token rather than your wallet address or email. The server stores
a hash of that token and its expiry against the invite record. The cookie is `HttpOnly`, so
scripts on the page cannot read it, uses `SameSite=Strict`, and is `Secure` in production.

### Server logs

Our hosting provider, Vercel, records standard request logs including IP addresses, for security and to keep the sites running. We do not use these logs for analytics and do not combine them with anything else.

### YouTube videos

The roadmap on everstrat.xyz shows monthly recap videos hosted on YouTube.

The video player is not loaded with the page. It is created only at the moment you press play. Until you do, your browser makes no request to YouTube or to any other Google service, whatever you chose on the consent banner.

Once you press play, YouTube receives your IP address and may set its own cookies, exactly as if you had opened the video on youtube.com. That is Google's processing, under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Wallet connections

When you connect a wallet to app.everstrat.xyz, your wallet address becomes visible to the services the app needs to read the blockchain and submit your transaction:

| Service | What it receives |
| --- | --- |
| Alchemy | Your address and the calls the app makes to read chain state |
| Goldsky | Queries against the Everstrat subgraph |
| WalletConnect | Connection data, if you connect through WalletConnect rather than a browser extension |

Accepting an invite also stores your wallet address on our server and links it to the invite's
email address when one is attached, as described in [Invite access and session cookie](#invite-access-and-session-cookie).
We do not send that wallet address or email to analytics services.

### Blockchain data

Transactions you sign are written to the Ethereum mainnet blockchain. That record is public, permanent and outside anyone's control, including ours.

We cannot edit it, delete it, or make it private. This is a property of public blockchains rather than a choice we have made, and no request under any law can change it. Consider it carefully before transacting.

## Cookies

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes visitors and sessions. Only set if you accept. | Up to 2 years |
| `eve_invite_access` | Everstrat | Keeps your verified invite session signed in to the restricted app area | 14 days |
| Consent choice | Everstrat | Remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics, so we stop asking | Stored in your browser, until you clear it |

The consent choice is kept in your browser's local storage rather than in a cookie, and never leaves your device.

The three sites are separate domains, so each asks you separately. Answering on one does not answer for the others.

## Why we are allowed to do this

For readers in the EU and UK, our legal bases under the GDPR are:

| What | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Analytics | Your consent, which you may withdraw at any time |
| Launch updates email | Your consent, given by submitting the form |
| Abuse-prevention hashes | Legitimate interest in keeping the signup form usable |
| Invite access cookie | Strictly necessary for a service you asked for |

## How long we keep things

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Launch updates email | Until we send the launch announcement, or until you ask us to remove it, whichever comes first |
| Abuse-prevention hashes | 12 months |
| Analytics | 14 months, after which Google deletes it |
| Invite access cookie and session validity | 14 days |
| Invite records, including wallet and any linked email | Retained beyond invite and session expiry; expiry does not automatically delete these records |

Clearing the invite cookie or letting the session expire does not delete the off-chain invite
record. To request deletion of invite data, contact **ping@everstrat.xyz**.

## Withdrawing consent

To stop analytics after accepting, clear your browser's site data for the site in question. The banner will appear again and you can decline.

Declining is remembered. We do not re-prompt people who have said no.

To have your email removed from the launch list, write to **ping@everstrat.xyz** from that address, or with it in the message. We will delete the record and confirm.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK, you have the right to ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable form. Similar rights apply in several other jurisdictions.

This includes launch-list emails and off-chain invite records, which may link an email address
to a wallet address. Write to **ping@everstrat.xyz** and we will act on your request within 30 days.
