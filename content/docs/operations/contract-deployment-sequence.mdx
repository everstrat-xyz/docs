## Fresh deployment

`script/ProtocolDeployBase.sol` in `everstrat-xyz/contracts` constructs:

1. Registry.
2. EVE and the ExitQueue implementation/proxy.
3. Controller, Oracle, StrategyManager, and Converter implementations/proxies.
4. Whitelist.
5. AMM.

Initialization binds modules to the Registry. Registering peers, configuring feeds/routes,
granting roles, and verifying parameters are separate setup steps.

Deploy the timelock and keeper executors, register them, and finalize ADMIN/SECURITY,
MINTER, and KEEPER grants. Remove temporary deployer authority as specified by the deployment
scripts. Set the treasury and performance fee explicitly; current production scripts do not
silently default these environment inputs.

## Automation activation

Keeper executors deploy without allowed `perform` callers.
Create/configure Mimic tasks and allowlist their exact smart account through ADMIN.
Deploying a contract or publishing a function alone does not activate automation.

## Strategies and later upgrades

Converters and token feeds must support the strategies' assets and routes.
A strategy is deployed separately and contributes to the basket only after registration.

Existing deployments use governance upgrade/registration operations rather than repeating
the entire fresh-deployment sequence. See [Current deployments](/operations/current-deployments)
for the Controller upgrade and v2 strategy migration.
