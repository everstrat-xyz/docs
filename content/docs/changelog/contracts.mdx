## 2026-10-12

### Governance can now withdraw from one strategy

Controller 1.1.0 is active on mainnet, giving the admin timelock a targeted withdrawal path for strategy migrations.

- The Controller proxy address stays the same.
- Keeper access remains available; SECURITY gains no new withdrawal permission.



<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Controller 1.1.0 activated

**Executed on mainnet.** Upgraded the Controller proxy to 1.1.0. The admin timelock can now call
`withdrawFromStrategy` alongside the keeper, providing a governed path to drain a specific strategy
for migration. SECURITY does not gain that permission. The proxy address remains unchanged.

Evidence: [contracts #52](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/contracts/pull/52),
[governance 018 payload](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/governance/tree/047f8e30136d127851a59c41cadf9f38e391a379/018-controller-upgrade-v1.1.0), and
[execution receipt](https://etherscan.io/tx/0xd3e005e636591e9c91c9c237be11d778baa1b31a18ca6b7af4ddb3750a9e2c33),
block **26,176,319**, **12:13:23 UTC**. The receipt contains the implementation upgrade and
timelock execution events. The proposal's older Scheduled wording predates this execution.

</details>

## 2026-09-26

### The 15% performance fee is enabled

The protocol now charges 15% of eligible accrued Uniswap V3 LP trading fees.

- The fee is settled by minting EVE to the DAO treasury.
- Deposits and general NAV growth are not the fee basis.



<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Performance fee enabled

**Executed on mainnet.** Changed `StrategyManager.performanceFeeBps` from **0** to **1500** (15%).
The fee applies to eligible accrued Uniswap V3 LP fees and is settled by minting EVE to the DAO
treasury. It is not a fee on deposits or all NAV growth.

Evidence: [governance 014](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/governance/tree/047f8e30136d127851a59c41cadf9f38e391a379/014-strategy-manager-performance-fee-bps),
[execution receipt](https://etherscan.io/tx/0xcac94cc8af126797663f909c10e10e40a42513b17d2d0c0fc1a3ecd03c74673a),
block **26,061,384**, **11:39:47 UTC**. See [Performance fee](/how-everstrat-works/strategies-and-yield#performance-fee).

</details>

</ChangeEntry>

## 2026-08-18

### Keeper receipts report what actually moved

Controller methods return achieved amounts so keeper receipts reflect execution results.

- Unpriced, cancellable exits no longer drive strategy-withdrawal liabilities.
- Empty whole-batch processing becomes a no-op.
- Deposit eligibility checks the strategy’s deposit weight.



<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Keeper liabilities and achieved amounts

**Merged.** Excluded unpriced, cancellable exits from strategy-keeper withdrawal liabilities.
Controller deposit, withdrawal, and harvest methods return achieved amounts, which the strategy
executor uses in receipts instead of requested estimates. Empty whole-batch processing becomes a
no-op, and deposit eligibility also checks nonzero deposit weight.

Source: [contracts #43](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/contracts/pull/43).
The returned amounts change the Controller ABI; clients should use the matching interfaces.

</details>

</ChangeEntry>

## 2026-08-17

### Share pricing accounts for priced exits

Live pricing and fee minting now account for in-window priced redemption liabilities and escrowed EVE.

- Minting happens before ETH is forwarded to Controller.
- Deployment checks enforce the timelock floor, proposer, and whitelist setup.



<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Priced-queue share accounting and deployment checks

**Merged.** Live pricing and fee minting deduct in-window priced redemption liabilities and their
escrowed EVE. Unpriced requests remain equity; expired unprocessed requests stop contributing to
those offsets and recover through cancellation/closure. Added a 25-live-priced-batch bound.
The ExitQueue storage-layout change requires migration planning for an initialized proxy.

Also reordered minting before the ETH transfer to Controller, and hardened deployment checks for
the 48-hour timelock floor, a nonzero proposer, and Whitelist registration before finalization.

Sources: [contracts #39](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/contracts/pull/39),
[#16](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/contracts/pull/16), and
[#41](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/contracts/pull/41).
Strategy circuit-breaker changes are recorded in [Strategy updates](/changelog/strategies#2026-08-17).

</details>

</ChangeEntry>
