## 2026-10-14

### See protocol earnings in Metrics

Metrics now shows the protocol’s available fee earnings in ETH and USD.

- Protocol Earnings joins NAV and deployed capital in Protocol overview.
- Pending Redemptions moves to Market & liquidity.
- Snapshot sharing moves to Admin Insights; existing shared links still work.

<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Protocol Earnings and Admin snapshot sharing

**Production deployment.** Metrics gained Protocol Earnings in ETH with a USD equivalent. Pending
Redemptions moved to Market & liquidity, and the Instant Liquidity headline card was removed.
Copy snapshot link moved to the Campaign link builder in authenticated Admin Insights. Existing
public snapshot URLs and image routes remain available.

Source: [app #15](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/pull/15),
[production commit 47c0756](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/commit/47c07562714ba31bc7cda9f8026c521550f544c6).

fees and attributable harvested EVE; see [Metrics](/using-everstrat/tracking-your-position).

## 2026-10-10

### Your Portfolio, separate from protocol Metrics

Follow your own position in Portfolio and the whole protocol in Metrics.

- Switch your position chart between Return and Value, in USD or ETH.
- View EVE market history with clearer loading and data-freshness feedback.
- Mint-form scrolling and long error messages display correctly.

<ChangeScreenshot name="portfolio">

**1. Return / Value and USD / ETH controls. 2. Position history.** Preview captured during chart development, using example data.

</ChangeScreenshot>

<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Portfolio and Metrics analytics

**Production deployment.** Separated personal Portfolio from protocol Metrics, preserving redirects
from the former dashboard/stats routes. Added connected-wallet position history with Return/Value
and USD/ETH controls, EVE market history, and clearer loading, stale-data, and error states.
Queued exits remain in the position until processing changes the holding.

The development toolchain also moved to **Node.js 24** and **Bun 1.4.3**, with read-only checks separated from autofixes. Source: [tooling commit 3f5bb71](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/commit/3f5bb71381).

Source: [app #2](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/pull/2), production commit `d4cf1f5`,
deployment `6978600729` (success). The data work is described in
[Data & Integrations](/changelog/data-and-integrations#2026-10-10).
Real connected-wallet reconciliation and visual acceptance remained tracked in
[app #12](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/issues/12); deployment success alone does not resolve it.

#### Mint-form and branding fixes

**Production deployment.** Fixed mint-form scrolling and long error-message wrapping, and
standardized Everstrat branding across headers.

Source: [app #7](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/pull/7), production commit `860fec9`,
deployment `6986524734` (success).

## 2026-10-08

### Wallet connections can recover

imToken no longer waits indefinitely for a permissions response.

- Other injected wallets show a retryable timeout after 60 seconds.
- WalletConnect keeps its QR connection flow.



<ChangeScreenshot name="wallet">

**1. WalletConnect** remains available in the wallet chooser alongside detected injected wallets.

Captured during development of this recovery fix on October 6. This image shows the connection interface; the timeout and retry behavior is described above.

</ChangeScreenshot>

<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Wallet connection recovery

**Production deployment.** imToken requests accounts directly instead of waiting indefinitely for
`wallet_requestPermissions`. Other injected wallets receive a retryable 60-second timeout;
WalletConnect retains its QR flow. Real imToken Android validation remained an explicit follow-up.

Source: [app #1](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/pull/1), production commit `669ab83`,
deployment `6930028485` (success). This supersedes the original monorepo PR after extraction.

</details>

</ChangeEntry>

## 2026-09-17

### Know the minimum before you queue an exit

The exit form shows how much EVE is needed to meet the queued-exit minimum.

- The displayed minimum rounds upward.
- A small quote buffer helps with NAV movement before submission.



<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Queued-exit minimum and NAV-movement buffer

**Committed.** The exit form shows the minimum EVE needed to queue an exit and rounds the displayed
minimum upward. Requested ETH is buffered to reduce failures from small NAV movements between the
quote and submission. These changes do not guarantee an immediate exit.

Sources: [a3c3350](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/commit/a3c3350b61),
[35afae5](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/commit/35afae54b6), and
