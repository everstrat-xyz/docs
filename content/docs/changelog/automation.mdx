## 2026-09-24

### Gas budgets depend on the job

W2’s new fee policy gives each action its own budget and increases urgency as priced exits approach expiry.

- Withdrawals and rebalances use deadline-aware ceilings.
- Deposits, harvests, and liquidity top-ups use amount-based budgets.
- Operators must redeploy the function and recreate its trigger.



<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Per-action W2 gas-fee policy

**Merged.** Replaced one flat fee policy with action-specific ceilings. Withdrawal shortfalls use
a deadline ramp, rebalances can inherit that urgency, sync uses a lower ceiling, and deposit,
harvest, and exit-liquidity actions use an amount-based budget. The function continues to forward
the on-chain checker's payload bytes. Invalid fee inputs produce configuration-error logs.

Source: [keepers #30](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/keepers/pull/30).
**Operator action:** deploy the new function and recreate the trigger with its updated inputs.
The merge date does not establish when that trigger became active.

</details>

</ChangeEntry>

## 2026-09-19

### Skip rebalance batches that can only revert

W2 skips a rebalance when every selected strategy is non-calm, avoiding guaranteed no-op work.

- Read failures are logged separately and emit no intent.
- Other actions keep the on-chain checker’s exact payload.



<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Suppress guaranteed no-op rebalance batches

**Merged.** W2 suppresses a Rebalance intent when every selected strategy is non-calm and the
batch would therefore only revert. Read failures emit no intent and are logged separately.
Other actions retain the checker's exact payload, and ambiguous payloads retain the prior relay
behavior. This is an off-chain mitigation for static strategies with the older health predicate.

Source: [keepers #28](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/keepers/pull/28).
**Operator action:** redeploy the function and recreate its trigger against the new CID.
The subsequent [strategy health-predicate fix](/changelog/strategies#2026-09-22) is a separate
implementation change.

</details>

</ChangeEntry>

## 2026-09-17

### Mimic is authorized to call both executors

The Mimic smart account is added to the queue and strategy executor allowlists.

- This authorizes executor calls without granting the account a Registry keeper role.
- Authorization alone does not establish continuous off-chain task execution.



<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Mimic caller authorized on both executors

**Executed on mainnet.** Added the Mimic smart account to the QueueKeeperExecutor and
StrategyKeeperExecutor caller allowlists. The smart account can call executor `perform()`;
it does not itself acquire Registry's KEEPER role. This authorization does not prove ongoing
task availability or a particular function CID.

Evidence: [governance 012](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/governance/tree/047f8e30136d127851a59c41cadf9f38e391a379/012-keeper-executors-allow-mimic-caller).

executed at block **25,997,085**, **12:02:59 UTC**; the
[strategy executor call](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x08760b797858c72e59a5b52c3b60ffdf85896f6614a9205be777c76775e0df3f)

## 2026-08-26

### Queue and strategy keepers move to Mimic

W1 and W2 are rebuilt as Mimic functions. W1 handles queue decisions; W2 relays the contract checker’s instructions.

- Timestamp conversion, missing-map reads, and payload guards are fixed.
- The old W4 watcher and Go plane are removed from the final merge.



<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Mimic W1/W2 functions

**Merged.** Replaced the earlier CRE/Gelato implementations with Mimic functions compiled from
AssemblyScript to WASM. W1 retains queue decisions and cross-checks; W2 relays the contract
checker's payload. Fixed millisecond/second conversion, missing-map access, AMM pause checks,
and payload-length enforcement. Removed the old W4 freeze-watch and Go plane from the final merge.

Sources: [keepers #24](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/keepers/pull/24),
[W4 removal 24534c3](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/keepers/commit/24534c3852), and
[W1 guards 7c58f2f](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/keepers/commit/7c58f2f378).
The final merged tree supersedes the PR description's earlier statement that W4 remained.
On-chain executor metadata changed in [contracts #46](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/contracts/pull/46);
caller authorization followed [on September 17](#2026-09-17).

</details>

</ChangeEntry>

## 2026-08-25

### Queue scans spend their read budget on useful work

Historical CRE keepers skip unusable queue candidates, avoid pricing after a truncated scan, and split large user-list reads into chunks.





<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### CRE-era queue selection and read-budget fixes

**Merged.** W1 skips expired candidates and continues to later processable batches instead of
spending the read budget on an unusable head. A truncated request scan blocks new pricing.
W2 excludes unpriced/expired batches from its needs scan and chunks large user-list reads.
These entries describe the historical CRE implementation before the Mimic migration.

Sources: [keepers #21](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/keepers/pull/21) and
[#22](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/keepers/pull/22).

</details>
