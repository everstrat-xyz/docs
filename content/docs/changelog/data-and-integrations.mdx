## 2026-10-10

### Position history follows your actual EVE holdings

Portfolio reconstructs positions from indexed mints, burns, transfers, and redemption-price snapshots.

- Queued exits stay in the position until processing changes the holding.
- Valuation uses lifetime prices, and usable history survives refresh failures.



<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Connected-wallet position reconstruction

**Merged.** Reconstructs EVE holdings from indexed mints, burns, transfers, and redemption-price
snapshots. Queued and slippage-closed exits remain part of the position until processing changes
the holding. Position history uses lifetime prices for valuation and preserves usable history
through refresh failures. The user-facing rollout is recorded in
[App updates](/changelog/app#2026-10-10).

Source: [app #2](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/pull/2), including
[e3d29f7](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/commit/e3d29f7cbf).
Synthetic checks and deployment success do not establish the outstanding real-wallet acceptance
tracked in [app #12](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/issues/12).

</details>

</ChangeEntry>

## 2026-09-30

### Missing data stays missing

Insights stops treating incomplete collection as complete results.

- Funnels preserve missing values and respect the selected period.
- Revised reports replace earlier versions atomically.
- Partial Telegram collection is shown separately.



<details className="change-details">
<summary>Details & sources</summary>

#### Insights completeness and attribution corrections

**Committed.** Incomplete protocol index coverage is rejected instead of treated as complete.
Conversion funnels preserve missing data and respect the selected period; weekly metrics retain
unknown values. Revised analytics reports replace prior versions atomically, and partial Telegram
collection is distinguished from complete collection.

Sources: [ebcef8f](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/commit/ebcef8f95b),
[e10f1a0](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/commit/e10f1a0882),
[599ab54](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/commit/599ab54331), and
[85030fc](https://github.com/everstrat-xyz/app/commit/85030fced1).

</details>

</ChangeEntry>
