## Current Behavior

- wagmi provides connection state; the app owns its wallet selection and account dialogs
- the dialog lists detected browser wallets and WalletConnect when that connector is configured
- the app is configured for Ethereum mainnet only
- connecting on an unsupported chain shows a **Wrong network** button; the user selects it
  and approves the Ethereum switch in their wallet
- connected users see a custom account dialog

Connecting does not by itself grant app access. During the invite period, `/mint`, `/portfolio`,
and `/metrics` require an access session and otherwise redirect to `/`. Invite redemption
requires a wallet-signed message and links access to that wallet. First mint can attach the
invite voucher through `AMM.enterWithInvite(...)`.

## Why The Single-Chain Assumption Matters

The app is not built as a generic chain router. Several UX paths, contract addresses, and explorer
links assume a single deployment snapshot.
