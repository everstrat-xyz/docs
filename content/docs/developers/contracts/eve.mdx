## Privileged supply functions

```solidity
function mint(address to, uint256 amount) external
function burn(uint256 amount) external
function burnFrom(address account, uint256 amount) external
```

All three require MINTER. At the snapshot, both **AMM and StrategyManager** hold that role.
AMM mints on entry and burns on redemption. StrategyManager mints treasury EVE when
harvesting eligible performance fees.

EVE is a transferable protocol share. Holding it grants no governance role. Its ETH redemption
price depends on net NAV and live supply, and can decrease.

## App and indexer integration

The burn flow may require approval to AMM. The subgraph records EVE approvals with
`spender == AMM`, alongside protocol mint/burn and claim events.
Queued EVE stays in AMM escrow until processed or cancelled.
