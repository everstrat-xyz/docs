## Responsibilities

StrategyManager registers strategies, computes ETH-denominated NAV, routes capital through the
Controller, and settles performance fees by minting EVE to the DAO treasury.

## Allocation and withdrawal weights

Governance sets separate `depositWeight` and `withdrawalWeight` values for each registered strategy.
Each is an integer in **0..100**. Zero excludes a strategy from the corresponding batch allocation.
The values are relative weights; they need not sum to 100 and are not safety scores or priorities.

Deposit allocation also considers capacity, health, pause state, and deposit cooldown.
Withdrawal capacity can prevent an allocation from being fully satisfied. Range-based operations
use `[startIndex, endIndex)`.

Only the Controller address registered under `Auth.CONTROLLER` can call routine deposit,
withdrawal, rebalance, sync, and harvest entry points. Governance controls registration and weights.

## NAV and live supply

`totalNAVInETH()` includes strategy NAVs, StrategyManager ETH, Controller ETH, AMM free ETH, and
the oracle value of supported ERC-20 balances held by StrategyManager. It deducts in-window
priced redemption liabilities. A required valuation failure propagates.

The AMM and performance-fee mint calculation use total EVE supply minus the matching priced
escrow. See [EVE & NAV](/how-everstrat-works/eve-and-nav) for the full accounting model.

## Removal and recovery

`removeStrategy()` requires reported NAV to be at most **10 wei** and propagates a reverting
NAV read. `forceRemoveStrategy()` can deregister a strategy despite a failed NAV read or
remaining assets. It records the reported NAV or read failure in an event. Deregistration
removes the strategy from accounting; it does not recover funds by itself.

## Performance fees

The [dated mainnet snapshot](/operations/current-deployments) has a **15%** rate, capped in code
at **20%**. Strategies report accrued LP trading fees in ETH terms. StrategyManager mints EVE
to `daoTreasury` using `feeETH × liveSupply / (NAV - feeETH)`.
See [Fees & costs](/using-everstrat/fees-and-costs).
