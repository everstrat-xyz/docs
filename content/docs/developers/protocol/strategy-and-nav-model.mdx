## Accounting

```text
gross assets = strategy NAVs + StrategyManager ETH + Controller ETH
               + AMM free ETH + supported ERC-20 value held by StrategyManager
net NAV = gross assets - in-window priced queued ETH liabilities
live supply = total EVE supply - matching priced queued EVE
base price = net NAV / live supply
```

AMM free balance excludes ETH already locked for processed claims. Unpriced queued EVE remains
equity. Priced outstanding requests are excluded during their processing window; those offsets
lapse after expiry. See [EVE & NAV](/how-everstrat-works/eve-and-nav).

See the [NAV and EVE pricing diagram](/how-everstrat-works/eve-and-nav#everstrat-nav-diagram).

## Strategy allocation

Governance sets independent deposit and withdrawal weights, each in `0..100`. A zero weight
excludes the strategy from that batch path. Routine operations come from the registered
Controller, subject to strategy capacity and eligibility.

## Valuation dependency

AMM price functions consume protocol NAV in ETH. They do not directly request an ETH/USD quote
for normal entry or exit; asset valuation can require Oracle feeds and pool TWAP data.
A required NAV read failure can block minting, redemption, and batch pricing.
