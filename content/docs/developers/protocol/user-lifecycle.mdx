## Lifecycle Stages

<Steps>
  <Step>Connect a wallet on Ethereum mainnet.</Step>
  <Step>Mint EVE by depositing ETH into the AMM.</Step>
  <Step>Hold or later burn EVE through the AMM redemption path.</Step>
  <Step>If liquidity is insufficient, wait for queue pricing and processing or cancel when permitted.</Step>
  <Step>Claim credited ETH after successful queued processing.</Step>
  <Step>Review current state and actions in Portfolio, and confirmed events in Activity.</Step>
</Steps>

## Indexed Outcomes

The subgraph maps user-visible lifecycle events into:

- `APPROVE`
- `BOOTSTRAP_MINT`
- `MINT`
- `BURN_IMMEDIATE`
- `BURN_QUEUED`
- `BURN_PROCESSED`
- `CANCEL_REDEMPTION`
- `CLAIM`

## Important Constraint

The frontend checks current-batch state and all discovered indexed pending batch IDs through RPC.
Pending discovery is independent of Activity pagination. The subgraph supplies history;
current balances and action eligibility come from RPC.
