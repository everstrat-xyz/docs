<Callout type="warn" title="Real ETH at risk">
  Everstrat is an experimental, unaudited protocol in a **testing phase on Ethereum mainnet**.
  Treat it as a test protocol. Deposits are real ETH and can be lost entirely.
  An invite grants access only. It does not mean the protocol is safe.
</Callout>

If you are new to the product, read [What is Everstrat?](/introduction/what-is-everstrat)
and [Core concepts](/introduction/core-concepts-glossary) first.

## Check before connecting

- **Network:** use Ethereum mainnet, chain id **1**. Testnet ETH cannot fund mainnet transactions.
- **Access:** entry requires an invite while the invite period is active. Follow the app's access
  flow before attempting your first deposit. For personal invitations, see
  [joining through a friend](/using-everstrat/referrals#join-through-a-friend).
- **Wallet and gas:** have ETH for the deposit **and** transaction gas. Approval, exit, and Claim
  are separate transactions when required. Leave ETH in your wallet instead of depositing its
  entire balance; the needed gas amount varies with network conditions.
- **Official deployment:** verify the full addresses against
  [Contract addresses](/reference/contract-addresses) and the dated
  [current deployment snapshot](/operations/current-deployments). Ignore unofficial token listings
  and unsolicited deposit instructions.
- **Risks and costs:** read [Key risks](/risk-and-trust/key-risks),
  [Fees & costs](/using-everstrat/fees-and-costs), and the [Risk Disclosure](/legal/risk-disclosure).
  Do not continue if the app's risk or restricted-person acknowledgment is not true for you.

Exits depend on pause state, valuation, liquidity, and queue rules. Queued settlement has no
guaranteed deadline. The detailed [exit guide](/using-everstrat/exiting-and-redeeming) explains
pending requests, credited ETH, and cancellation conditions.
