<Callout type="warn" title="Testing phase — treat as a test protocol">
  Everstrat is deployed on **Ethereum mainnet** (chain id **1**), but it is in a **testing phase**
  and should be treated as a **test protocol**. Entry is invite-gated and the software is
  experimental and unaudited. Deposits are real ETH — do not deposit more than you can afford to
  lose entirely.
</Callout>

Everstrat lets you deposit ETH to receive EVE tokens, which represent your share of managed on-chain
strategies. Keepers run routine queue and strategy operations through permissioned executor
contracts. At the
[dated deployment snapshot](/operations/current-deployments#exit-liquidity-at-this-block),
new exits queue, and you claim ETH in a separate transaction once your request is processed.
This site documents the product, protocol, and operational details for users, integrators,
and contributors.
